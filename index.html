About Orbit

Orbit is a one-person studio founded and run by Lily Dubin — a software developer who designs, builds, and ships every app end-to-end: architecture, UI, data layer, and the app icon. No team, no outsourcing — every line in every Orbit app is written, tested, and maintained solo.

Orbit's apps share a philosophy: do one thing well, keep it fully offline where possible, and never ask for more than the app needs. Each product page below goes into the real engineering behind that — bugs found and fixed, data migrated without loss, and how each app is tested before it ships.
